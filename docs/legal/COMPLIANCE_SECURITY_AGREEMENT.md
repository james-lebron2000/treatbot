# 合规与安全协议 / Compliance & Security Agreement

版本 / Version: 2025-12-18

## 中文
1. 本产品用于临床试验匹配与临床信息结构化提取。使用者应确保其拥有合法授权处理相关医疗数据。
2. 发送至第三方模型/服务的内容必须不包含患者可识别的个人信息（例如：姓名、电话、身份证号、住址、病案号等）。
3. 系统会对文本与结构化输入做去标识化处理以降低风险，但仍建议使用者在上传前进行必要的脱敏核查。

## English
1. This product is for clinical trial matching and clinical information extraction. You must have legal authorization to process any medical data you provide.
2. Any content sent to third-party models/services must not include direct patient identifiers (e.g., name, phone number, ID number, address, medical record number).
3. The system applies de-identification to reduce risk, but you should still verify redaction before upload.
